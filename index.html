Follow-ups that run themselves.

Six small automations that send the message your team forgets to send.

  • On form submit

    Instant reply

    Someone fills in your form and gets a real reply within a minute, not "we'll be in touch".

  • Before each booking

    No-show reminders

    A reminder the day before and another that morning, with a one-tap confirm.

  • 3 days after service

    Review request

    Every customer gets the same polite ask for a rating and a review, whether you remembered or not.

  • On a missed call

    Missed-call text-back

    When a call rings out, the caller gets a message seconds later asking what they need.

  • After 6 months away

    Reactivation

    Customers who haven't been back in six months get one message inviting them back. Sent only to people who agreed to hear from you.

  • 7 days unpaid

    Invoice nudge

    If an invoice is still unpaid after seven days, a polite reminder goes out on its own.

FAQ

Questions owners ask.

Is this done by AI?

Yes. AI agents build the pages, write the replies and prepare the ads. A person checks every piece before it goes live, and you approve everything.

Who pays for the ads?

You pay ad spend directly to Meta on your own ad account. Our fee covers the build and management.

Do you need access to our WhatsApp?

We set up auto-replies on your WhatsApp Business number with your team. You keep full control and can switch them off any time.

What about regulated ads?

Some UAE ads need approval first: health ads from the health authority, property ads a Trakheesi permit. You stay the advertiser, and we prepare everything for your sign-off before anything runs.

Do you work in Arabic?

Yes. Pages and replies can be in English, Arabic or both.

Have you worked with businesses like mine?

We're a new studio. That's why every pitch starts with a free draft for your business, so you can judge the work before paying.
