FAQ

Questions clinics ask.

Is this done by AI?

Yes. AI agents build the pages, write the replies and prepare the ads. A person checks every piece before it goes live, and you approve everything.

Who pays for the ads?

Your clinic pays ad spend directly to Meta on your own ad account. Our fee covers the build and management.

Do you need access to our WhatsApp?

We set up auto-replies on your WhatsApp Business number with your team. You keep full control and can switch them off any time.

What about health advertising rules?

Health ads in the UAE can need approval from the relevant health authority. Your clinic stays the advertiser, and we prepare everything for your sign-off before anything runs.

Do you work in Arabic?

Yes. Pages and replies can be in English, Arabic or both.

Have you worked with clinics before?

We're a new studio. That's why every pitch starts with a free draft for your clinic, so you can judge the work before paying.
